# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Agent Browser respectful and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect differing experience, context, and communication styles.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing sensitive reproduction material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information
without permission is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement
decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Use GitHub's report-content tools for platform abuse. When a private maintainer conversation is
needed, contact AetherAI3 through a private contact method listed on its GitHub organization
profile. If none is available, contact GitHub Support rather than placing sensitive conduct
details in a public repository thread. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
Deliberately false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person publicly represents the project. It is
adapted from the community values expressed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, licensed under
